4. About KCYF

The Knocking Collaborators Youth Foundation is a youth-led organization working across Bangladesh to develop communication and leadership capability among students. Our work to date includes free public speaking workshops conducted at institutions nationwide, the National Virtual Public Speaking Competition 2022, the KCYF Intra Debate Competition, and a child education program supporting 100+ students with educational accessories.

5. About the Public Speaking Carnival

The KCYF Public Speaking Carnival is our flagship annual initiative, now entering its 3rd edition. It combines free workshops conducted across schools and colleges with a culminating hybrid event featuring competitions, workshops, speech sessions, cultural programs, and a prize-giving ceremony. Previous editions have engaged 500+ direct participants and partnered with 20+ reputed brands and institutions, including Yes Academy, Torongo Coaching Centre, Metropolitan University, EMK Center, and T2 Care.
